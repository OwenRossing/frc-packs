About FRC Packs

The 2026 Championship pack holds the 515 teams from Houston's 8 divisions, with EPA and records from Statbotics. Mythic is the season's top 50 teams by EPA who made it to Houston; the rest are tiered by their EPA at Champs. Robot photos come from The Blue Alliance; the few teams without one get a drawing. Every pack, serial number and free-pack timer is decided by the server, and your cards are saved to your account, so you can sign in from any device.
