Privacy

Last updated October 9, 2026

FRC Packs is a free card game for the FIRST Robotics community, run by volunteers. We collect only what the game needs to work. There are no ads, no tracking or analytics scripts, and we never sell or share your information.

What we keep

WhatWhy
Your username and the invite code you joined withSo you can sign in, and so other players can trade with you. Usernames are visible to other players.
Your password, stored only as a one-way hash (argon2)To check it when you sign in. Nobody, including the admin, can read it.
Your packs, cards, serial numbers, parts, trades, missions, streak, wishlist and showcaseThat's the game. Your profile (showcase, stats, wishlist) is visible to other signed-in players.
Reports you send about other playersSo the admin can deal with them. Only the admin sees reports.
A push subscription for each device where you turn on notificationsTo send "free pack ready" and trade notifications. Turn them off in Settings to delete it.

Cookies and storage

One cookie keeps you signed in. It's only used by FRC Packs. Your browser also stores a few preferences (sound, which tips you've seen, which rarities to scrap) on your device only.

Your IP address

To slow down password guessing, the server counts failed sign-ins per address for 15 minutes, in memory only. It isn't saved. The site is served through Cloudflare, which handles connections under its privacy policy.

Other services the page uses

  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when they load (Google's policy).
  • Notifications, if you turn them on, go through your browser's push service (Google, Mozilla or Apple).
  • Robot photos come from The Blue Alliance and team stats from Statbotics. They're copied onto our server, so your browser doesn't contact those sites.

Deleting your account

Ask the person who invited you or the site admin, and your account and all its cards are deleted. Serial numbers aren't reused. Database backups are kept for two weeks and then deleted.

Young players

FRC Packs is made for FIRST teams, and many players are students. We don't ask for real names, emails, ages or locations. Usernames are filtered for offensive words, and any player can be reported.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the game.
